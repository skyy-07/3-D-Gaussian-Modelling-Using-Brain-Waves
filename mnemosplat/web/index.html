The demo, honestly

One narrative. Two brain states.

Both conditions below receive the same transcript, same scene graph, same keyframe and same depth map. The only variable is the EEG-derived salience field. That is the whole experiment: same words, different memory.

01 Language → scene

Meta muse-spark-1.3 turns the transcript into entities, bounding boxes, depth hints and a beat sheet. muse-image-1.0 renders one photographic keyframe. sam-3.1 refines boxes to masks.

02 EEG → salience

Posterior theta, alpha, beta and gamma are reduced to band ratios, spectral entropy, certainty and lateralisation. The result is a 2D attention field. EEG never touches RGB pixels. It only sets opacity, spread and density.

03 Depth → Gaussians

Single-view monocular depth lifts the keyframe into surfels sized by their pixel footprint. Low-salience geometry becomes wide and translucent; vivid geometry stays tight and opaque. One keyframe is re-lifted per epoch, so the timeline scrubs consolidation, not camera motion.

Zero ground-truth leakage The renderer never sees the target image of the memory. It sees a scene graph, a synthesised keyframe and a salience field. Neither condition is allowed a cleaner depth map, a better prompt or a different camera.

The two comparison conditions use synthetic EEG generated with realistic spectral statistics. You can also upload a recorded CSV or NPY file: it is labelled recorded EEG and changes only density, opacity and spread — never the image. This is a systems prototype, not a clinical instrument or a diagnostic. Real EEG, IRB approval and a held-out ground-truth protocol are required before any claim about PTSD or memory accuracy.

Recorded EEG · public demo

Put your brain in the room.

Condition re-lifts the same baked scene. EEG changes only density, opacity and spread — never RGB pixels. This is the controlled demo.

Drop an EEG file here CSV with channel columns, or NPY · max 12 MB

CSV — first row headers, one row per sample, for example time,O1,O2,Oz,P3,P4,Pz. A time column in seconds, milliseconds or microseconds overrides the sampling-rate field. NPY — a 2-D numeric array in (channels, samples) or (samples, channels) order. EDF/BDF are not supported yet; export CSV or NPY. At least 2.25 s (three 0.75 s windows) is required. Channel names O1/O2/P3/P4/Oz/Pz enable the hemifield prior.

Choose a recording to begin.

Recorded EEG is treated as research data, not clinical data. No diagnosis is made. Processing happens in this demo service and the generated volume is temporary: it disappears when the service restarts or redeploys.

BrainVis · experimental GPU branch

Static frames, not a decoded video.

These images were generated by the BrainVis cascade from independent 440 ms EEG windows. They are not temporally consistent, not a video, and not a memory. The model is trained on a 40-class EEG-Image benchmark; arbitrary recordings are out of distribution. Treat this as an experiment, never as evidence of what a person saw.

The CPU app only forwarded the recording to an external CUDA worker. It did not run Stable Diffusion itself. Outputs are session-only and are deleted when the service restarts.
